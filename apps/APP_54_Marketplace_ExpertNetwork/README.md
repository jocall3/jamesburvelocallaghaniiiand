# APP_54_Marketplace_ExpertNetwork

## Problem Statement

In today's rapidly evolving business landscape, organizations frequently require highly specialized domain expertise for critical initiatives such as due diligence, technical validation, strategic consulting, or market analysis. The traditional methods of sourcing these experts—headhunters, personal networks, or generic consulting firms—are often slow, opaque, expensive, and lack the agility required for modern enterprise demands. This leads to delayed decision-making, suboptimal project outcomes, and significant opportunity costs. There is a pressing need for a rigorous, efficient, and transparent platform that connects deal teams and project managers with vetted, high-quality domain experts, streamlining the entire engagement lifecycle from discovery to billing.
